# Privacy Policy for Evolvo

**Effective date:** Upon first publication of this policy  
**Developer:** varnio  
**Contact:** varnioworker@gmail.com

Evolvo is a free educational Android app for exploring the relationships between living things.

## Information stored on your device

Evolvo stores app preferences, such as your selected appearance and whether you have seen its introductory tip, on your device. Its bundled tree, images and educational information can be used offline.

Evolvo does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Online articles and external websites

When you open online information, Evolvo may display pages from Wikipedia, GBIF, Catalogue of Life, NCBI or WoRMS inside the app. Links may also open in your web browser.

These websites and their supporting services receive network requests from your device. Those requests can include your IP address, browser or WebView information, and the address of the requested page. Websites may also use cookies or local storage.

Evolvo disables third-party cookies in its embedded information view. JavaScript is disabled for Wikipedia pages and may be enabled for other supported providers.

Third-party services handle information according to their own privacy policies, including their rules for retention and deletion. Please consult the privacy policies linked on those websites.

## Children’s privacy

Evolvo is intended for children and adults. It does not ask children to create accounts or submit personal information.

Online articles and external links connect to third-party services as described above. Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of these online features.

## Contacting the developer

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message. We use this information to respond to your enquiry. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

## Managing your information

You can clear Evolvo’s local app data through Android settings or uninstall the app. Android’s backup and restore settings may affect whether preferences are restored.

For information held by a third-party website, contact that provider using its published privacy contact details.

## This policy website

This policy page is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may receive technical information such as your IP address.

See [GitHub’s Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details of its data handling.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Evolvo’s features or data handling change. The updated policy will be published at this address with a revised effective date.
